App Updates

Learn why updating apps is important and how to update them.

1

Open Play Store

Open the Google Play Store on your Android phone.

2

Open Manage Apps

Tap your profile picture and choose the option for managing apps and device.

3

Check Available Updates

Look for apps that have an Update button.

4

Tap Update

Tap Update and wait until the application is updated.

Why Are Updates Important?

Updates can improve security, fix problems and provide new features.
