UPI / Google Pay

Learn how to make a UPI payment safely.

1

Open your UPI app

Open Google Pay or another trusted UPI application on your phone.

2

Scan the QR code

Tap the QR scanner and scan the receiver's QR code.

3

Enter the amount

Enter the amount you want to pay and carefully check the receiver's name.

4

Enter your UPI PIN

Enter your UPI PIN only on the official payment screen. Never share your PIN or OTP with anyone.

Important Safety Tip

Never share your UPI PIN, OTP or password with anyone.
