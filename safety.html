Digital Safety

Learn simple ways to stay safe while using digital services.

1

Never Share Your OTP

An OTP is private. Never tell your OTP to another person, even if they claim to be from a bank or company.

2

Keep Your PIN Private

Never tell anyone your UPI PIN, ATM PIN or password.

3

Be Careful With Suspicious Links

Do not click unknown links received through SMS, WhatsApp or email.

4

Be Careful About Online Fraud

Before making a payment or sharing information, stop and verify who is asking for it.

Remember

Banks and genuine services will never ask you to share your OTP, PIN or password.
